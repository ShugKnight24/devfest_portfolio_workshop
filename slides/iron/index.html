Form Check

Bad Form: Ego Lifting
"Build me a full SaaS platform like Uber. Write the backend, frontend, and DB all at once."

-> Result: You get crushed by code you can't debug.
Good Form: Isolation Sets
"Step 1: Design the DB schema for auth. Wait for approval.
Step 2: Write the API route. Spot me on the form."

-> Result: Exact tension on the muscle. Flawless recovery.
NOTES: "Ego Lifting" with AI always fails. Show them how to isolate the component. Do one rep (schema), verify it, then do the next rep (routes). Maintain the Mind-Muscle connection with your architecture.

Hypertrophy

Tear it Down.

Muscle grows by tearing fibers. Codebases evolve the same way. You build great apps by letting AI write the prototype, actively breaking it, and forcing it to refactor stronger. Never skip leg day (Automated Testing).

NOTES: Hypertrophy. Don't be afraid to delete the code the AI gives you. If it wrote it in 10 seconds, you can afford to throw it away and demand a stronger architectural foundation.
